(`multi_param_types`, `edge_values`, `ambiguous_and_adversarial`), 25 scoreable prompts / 63
parameters.

|---|---|

| Parameter accuracy | 58/63 = **92%** |

First full accuracy measurement of the session, taken right after the initial round of bug fixes
(`TokenizerFactory` arity mismatch, the `parameter.valie` typo, negative-number support in
`IntState`/`FloatState`, the `--output` path-doubling fix). Known misses at this point: sign loss
on negative numbers in a couple of edge cases, an empty-string greeting bug, a quote-style swap on
embedded double quotes, and the prompt-injection prompt being successfully hijacked into calling
the wrong function with an attacker-supplied argument.
