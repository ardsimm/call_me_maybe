## What changed since `report_5`

`FloatState` gained three new stages (`POST_SCIENTIFIC_E_TOKEN`, `POST_SCIENTIFIC_SIGN`,
`POST_SCIENTIFIC_EXP`) branching off `POST_FLOAT_POINT` whenever an `e`/`E` token is picked, via a
`_advance_stage` override that special-cases `POST_FLOAT_POINT` (checking for `e`/`E` vs. a real
terminator) while deferring to the base class's generic transition logic everywhere else. The
grammar now requires an explicit `+`/`-` immediately after `e`/`E` (the model reliably supplies `+`
when the source has no explicit sign), then one or more exponent digits, then either more exponent
digits or termination.

One bug was caught and fixed along the way: `plus_token` was initially assigned
`self._tokenizer.encode("-")` (copy-paste of `minus_token`'s line) instead of `encode("+")`, so
`POST_SCIENTIFIC_E_TOKEN`'s allowed tokens were the minus token duplicated twice -- structurally
the *only* legal choice after `e`, regardless of what the model wanted. That exactly matched the
reported symptom ("qwen always picks the minus sign after the e"). Fixed to `encode("+")` and
verified: all 7 of the new `data/input/float_tests/` fixtures pass, including previously-broken
negative-exponent cases (`6.022e-23`, `-6.022e-23`).

Verified positive-exponent, negative-exponent, explicit-sign, and no-decimal-point-but-has-exponent
cases all now round-trip correctly:

| Prompt | Result |
|---|---|
| What is the sum of 6.022e23 and 1 | `a: 6.022e+23` ✓ |
| What is the sum of 6.022e+23 and 1 | `a: 6.022e+23` ✓ |
| What is the sum of 6.022e-23 and 1 | `a: 6.022e-23` ✓ |
| What is the sum of -6.022e-23 and 1 | `a: -6.022e-23` ✓ |

| What is the sum of -6.0 and 1 | `a: -6.0` ✓ |

## Still open: the no-decimal-point overflow gap

`extreme_edge_cases` item 7 (`"What is the sum of -99999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999
and 1"`, no decimal point in the source) still reproducibly (confirmed across multiple reruns)
falls back to the `42.0` placeholder instead of the correct `-1e+50`. Root cause, from the token
trace: `PRE_FLOAT_POINT` (the stage before any `.` has been emitted) has never included
`string_end_sequences` in its allowed tokens -- the grammar structurally requires passing through a
literal `.` before termination is even possible. For this prompt the model gets stuck emitting `9`
for the full `TOKEN_GEN_LIMIT` budget without choosing to emit a `.` in time, so the resulting
digit run overflows `float()` to `-inf`, caught cleanly by the `math.isinf` guard and replaced with
the placeholder -- no crash, no invalid JSON, just a wrong value. This is unrelated to the
scientific-notation work (`PRE_FLOAT_POINT`'s allowed/transition tokens are untouched by it) and
remains open.
